    Methodology

    Detection

    Five unsupervised models — Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM, Local Outlier Factor, Elliptic Envelope and k-nearest-neighbour distance — score every trading day. Scores are converted to percentile ranks and averaged; the top 5% of days are flagged. “Agreement” is how many of the five models individually placed the day in their own top 5%.

    Shock and regime

    The ensemble runs twice. Shock uses single-day features (return, opening gap, intraday range, return z-score) and asks whether the day itself was extreme. Regime uses 20- and 60-day trend and volatility features and asks whether the day sits inside an unusual period. A day can be both.

    Explanation

    For each flagged day, SEBI filings and Business Standard coverage dated near the day, plus Wikipedia background, are retrieved from a vector store and passed to a language model with instructions to cite only that context. Explanations are generated, not verified, and should be read alongside their sources.
