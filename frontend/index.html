    How it works

    From raw prices to an explained anomaly

    1. 01

      Detect

      Five unsupervised models — Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM, Local Outlier Factor, Elliptic Envelope and k-nearest-neighbour distance — score every trading day. Scores become percentile ranks and are averaged; the top 5% of days are flagged. Agreement is how many of the five models individually placed the day in their own top 5%.

    2. 02

      Classify

      The ensemble runs twice. Shock uses single-day features (return, opening gap, intraday range, return z-score): was the day itself extreme? Regime uses 20- and 60-day trend and volatility: is the day inside an unusual period? A day can be both.

    3. 03

      Explain

      SEBI filings and Business Standard coverage dated near the day, plus Wikipedia background, are retrieved from a vector store and passed to a language model that must cite only that material. Explanations are generated, not verified — read them alongside their sources.
